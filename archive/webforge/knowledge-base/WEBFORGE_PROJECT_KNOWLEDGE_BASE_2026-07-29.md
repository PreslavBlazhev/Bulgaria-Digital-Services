# CASE STUDY — MAKE UP BY DENITSA

## Контекст
Beauty brand и academy, която има нужда от собствена система за продажба на обучения.

## Проблем
- липса на centralized course platform;
- липса на customer accounts;
- липса на integrated payments;
- ограничено tracking;
- нужда от premium personal-brand presentation.

## Решение
- custom Node/Express application;
- Render deployment;
- SQLite persistent database;
- registration/login;
- course catalogue и purchase flow;
- DSK V-POS;
- success/fail payment handling;
- server-to-server verification;
- privacy/cookie updates;
- GA4 + Consent Mode v2;
- admin exclusion from analytics;
- automated analytics tests.

## Потвърдени технически резултати
- production environment и custom domain;
- payment integration flow;
- analytics implementation;
- 86/86 analytics tests successful;
- version-controlled deployment.

## Непотвърдени KPI
Не се твърдят точни ръстове на sales, leads или conversion, докато не се извлекат GA4, payment и CRM данни.

## Reusable assets
LMS architecture, V-POS pattern, payment pages, consent analytics, beauty academy layout, course purchase UX, deployment checklist.

## Upsell
Email funnel, abandoned checkout, referral, progress, certificates, membership, SEO content, monthly analytics.

# 09 — ЕКИП, AI И SCALE ДО 90% АВТОНОМНОСТ

## Пет системи
1. Finding Clients: targeting, sources, research, scoring, outreach, referrals, inbound.
2. Sales: qualification, discovery, packages, proposal, objections, follow-up, contract, deposit, handoff.
3. Delivery: templates, reusable code, PM, QA, launch, handover, maintenance.
4. Management: roles, capacity, KPI, review, risk, ownership, quality.
5. Finance: pricing, margins, cash, collections, MRR, hiring gates, forecast, budgets.

AI Operating Layer automates research, drafting, documentation, code, QA, reporting and repetitive operations.

## Team design

### Founder/CEO — Преслав
Future focus: strategy, key partnerships, capital allocation, product direction, weekly CEO review.  
Should not daily add every lead, write every follow-up, build every site, test everything or answer every ticket.

### Sales Coordinator / Closer
CRM, outreach, discovery, proposal, follow-up, close.

### Project Manager / Client Success
Kickoff, scope, timeline, client communication, blockers, acceptance, renewals.

### Site Builder / Full-stack
Implementation, integrations, tests, deploy, technical docs.

### Designer / Content
UI, brand, content formatting, assets, conversion consistency.

### QA / Support
Release checks, regression, incidents, maintenance.

### Finance/Admin
Contracts, invoices, collections, subscriptions, archive.

## Hiring sequence
1. Sales support/closer.
2. Site builder.
3. Designer/developer or PM according to bottleneck.
4. Support/QA.
5. Finance/admin.

If delivery is the proven bottleneck first, hire site builder before sales. Use data.

## AI allocation
**ChatGPT:** strategy, positioning, offers, SOP, KPI, finance, QA reasoning, CEO review.  
**Claude Cowork:** local docs, tables, emails, repeatable office operations.  
**Claude Code:** repositories, code, tests, refactoring, Git, deploy, technical automation.

## AI boundaries
No autonomous price exception, legal commitment, unchecked production deploy, unapproved sensitive-data flow, invented KPI/testimonial or deletion without backup/approval.

## Autonomy phases
0. Founder-driven.
1. Documented.
2. Delegated delivery.
3. Delegated sales.
4. Managed operations.
5. 90% autonomous: founder reviews weekly metrics, major hires, pricing, product and partnerships.

## Autonomy gate
- 80%+ lead records maintained without founder;
- 70%+ standard sales close without founder;
- 80%+ standard delivery without founder implementation;
- 95% release QA pass;
- 90% support resolved without founder;
- weekly report automated;
- no critical account held by only one person;
- cash/margin/pipeline visible;
- client satisfaction remains acceptable.

## Weekly CEO dashboard
Sales: new leads, contacts, replies, qualified, offers, wins, weighted pipeline.  
Delivery: projects, on-time, overdue, blockers, QA failures, utilization.  
Finance: collections, MRR, margin, receivables, runway, forecast.  
Clients: support, health, renewals, churn, testimonials, upsells.  
Systems: SOP gaps, automation failures, security, single points of failure.

## Decision rights
Team: routine work within package/SOP.  
PM/Lead: timeline shifts, revision classification, minor production changes, contractor allocation.  
Founder: discount >10%, contract exceptions, liability, large unplanned spend, strategic client, new product, hire/fire, high-risk architecture.

## Productization roadmap
1. Local Business Website Engine.
2. QR Menu Engine.
3. Restaurant Ordering OS.
4. Booking Engine.
5. Academy/LMS Engine.

Each needs fixed core, allowed custom zones, setup checklist, standard price/timeline, maintenance, demo, case study, training and support boundaries.
